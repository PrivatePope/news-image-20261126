# The World in One Day — 26 November 2026

A newspaper front page summing up Thursday 26 November 2026's world news in one image.

Entry for the make.fun bounty [Sum up 26 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261126) — $100, 50 places.

## The day's stories

All events happened, or were first reported, on Wednesday 25 November 2026
(overnight into Thursday 26 November) — the news of the 26 November front page.

- **Lead — Oil shock in the Gulf:** Yemen's Houthis said they attacked Saudi
  Arabia's Ras Tanura refinery, the kingdom's largest, and Riyadh's financial
  district. Saudi Arabia said its air defences shot down four drones over the
  site, with no injuries reported. Brent crude settled $3.21 higher at $94.61
  a barrel; WTI rose to $83.68. (Reuters, 25 Nov 2026)
- **Cairo — 31 killed as fuel tanker explodes:** A tanker carrying petroleum
  products crashed and burst into flames on Cairo's Ring Road in the Waraq
  district, engulfing more than two dozen cars. Egypt's health ministry put
  the toll at 31 dead and 21 injured. (AP / CNN, 25 Nov 2026)
- **Trade — US proposes tariffs on 14 economies:** Washington proposed
  Section 301 tariffs — up to 20% on Chinese goods and 15% on the EU, Japan,
  South Korea, Taiwan and Switzerland — over manufacturing overcapacity.
  Nothing takes effect yet. (USTR, 25 Nov 2026)
- **The lighter side — Brazil's bug army beats dengue:** Mosquitoes carrying
  the Wolbachia bacterium cut infections by 68.5% in children in the
  four-year EVITA trial in Belo Horizonte. (Emory University, presented
  21 Nov 2026, widely reported 24–25 Nov)

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the bundled
`entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output`).
The image itself was drawn in the build session; the repo ships the finished image.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```
